# Promotions and gift cards

## Discount codes
One discount code can be used per order. Codes cannot be combined and cannot be applied after the order was placed.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are valid for 12 months from purchase and can be used in several orders until the balance is spent.
They cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Price drop after purchase
We do not refund the difference if a price drops after your order. You can return the product within 14 days
and order it again at the new price.
