# Warranty

## Warranty period
All products have a 24-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. Monitors have 36 months.

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number and a description of the fault. Photos or a short video help.
We send a free DPD label; the device goes to the manufacturer's service centre.

## Repair time
The service centre has 14 days to repair or replace the device after it receives it. If repair is not possible,
you get a replacement or a refund.

## Dead on arrival
If a product does not work out of the box, report it within 7 days of delivery. We replace it directly from stock
without sending it to the service centre.

## What the warranty does not cover
Physical damage, liquid damage, normal wear such as worn keyboard keycaps, and faults caused by unofficial repairs.
