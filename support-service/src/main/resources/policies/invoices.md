# Invoices

## Receipts and invoices
Every order gets an electronic receipt by email. A VAT invoice is issued when you give a company name and tax number.

## Invoice for a company
Polish companies give their NIP number, EU companies a VAT-EU number. The invoice can be requested
up to 3 months after the end of the month in which the order was paid.

## Correcting an invoice
Wrong company details can be corrected within the same 3 months. A correction invoice is sent by email.
