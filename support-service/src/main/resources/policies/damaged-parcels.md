# Damaged or missing parcels

## Damaged parcel
If the box is visibly damaged, you may refuse it at the courier or report it in the InPost app.
Report damage to us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the box and the item.

## Wrong or missing item
If an item is missing from the parcel or a different item arrived, contact support within 7 days of delivery
with a photo of the parcel contents. We send the missing item or a return label at our cost.

## Lost parcel
If tracking shows no update for 5 working days, we open a claim with the carrier. When the carrier confirms
the loss, we send the order again or refund it, whichever you prefer.
