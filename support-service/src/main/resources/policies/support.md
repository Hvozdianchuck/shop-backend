# Customer support

## Opening hours
Support answers on working days from 8:00 to 18:00 Central European Time. Messages sent outside these hours
are answered on the next working day.

## Response time
We answer most messages within 1 working day. Complaints are handled by a senior agent within 3 working days.

## Languages
Support answers in Ukrainian, Polish and English.

## Complaints
If you are unhappy with how your case was handled, write to support and ask for a complaint review.
A senior agent reviews the case and answers within 14 days.
