# Delivery

## Shipping methods
We ship from our warehouse in Poland. In Poland you can choose an InPost Paczkomat parcel locker or a DPD courier.
To Ukraine we ship with Nova Poshta to a branch or a parcel locker. Other EU countries get a DPD courier.

## Delivery times
Orders confirmed on a working day before 14:00 leave the warehouse the same day.
Delivery takes 1-2 working days in Poland, 3-5 working days to other EU countries and 4-7 working days to Ukraine.
Delivery times are estimates from the carrier, not a guarantee.

## Shipping costs
Paczkomat costs 3 EUR and a DPD courier in Poland costs 5 EUR. Shipping to other EU countries costs 12 EUR,
to Ukraine 9 EUR. Orders over 100 EUR ship for free within Poland.

## Tracking
The tracking number is sent by email when the parcel leaves the warehouse. Until then the order shows as CONFIRMED.
If the tracking number does not work for 24 hours after it was sent, contact support.

## Customs for Ukraine
Parcels to Ukraine may be charged import duty and VAT by Ukrainian customs. These charges are paid by the customer
to Nova Poshta on collection and are not refunded by the shop.
