# Privacy

## What data we keep
We keep your name, address, email and phone number to deliver orders and handle returns, for 5 years for tax reasons.

## Access and deletion
You can ask for a copy of your data or for its deletion by contacting support. Deletion requests are handled
within 30 days. Order data that we must keep for tax reasons is kept until the legal period ends.

## Card details
We never see or store full card numbers. Card payments are handled by the payment provider.
Never send your card number to support.
