# Payments

## Payment methods
We accept BLIK, Visa and Mastercard cards, Przelewy24 bank transfers and Apple Pay. Cash on delivery is not available.
Customers in Ukraine can pay by card only.

## When you are charged
Card payments are authorised when the order is placed and charged when the order is confirmed.
BLIK and Przelewy24 payments are charged immediately. If the order is rejected because a product is out of stock,
the money is returned within 3 working days.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, the order is not created and nothing is charged. A BLIK code is valid for 2 minutes;
if it expired, place the order again with a new code.

## Currency
Prices are shown and charged in EUR. Your bank may convert the amount and charge a fee for it, which we do not control.

## Double charge
If you see two charges for one order, the second one is usually an authorisation that expires within 7 days.
If it is still there after 7 days, contact support with your order number and a bank statement.
