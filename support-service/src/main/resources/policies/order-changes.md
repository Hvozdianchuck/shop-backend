# Order changes and cancellation

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order while it is PENDING or CONFIRMED and has not left the warehouse. Cancellation is confirmed
by a support agent. After the parcel has shipped, use a return instead.

## Refund after cancellation
After a cancellation, the payment is returned within 3 working days to the payment method used for the order.

## Changing the delivery address
The address can be changed until the parcel leaves the warehouse. After that, contact the carrier
(InPost, DPD or Nova Poshta) with the tracking number.

## Changing products in an order
Products cannot be added to or removed from an existing order. Cancel the order and place a new one.

## Rejected orders
An order is REJECTED when a product went out of stock between placing and confirming the order.
Nothing is shipped and any payment is returned automatically.
